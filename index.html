Jinsu Park

Ph.D. Candidate in Business Administration, Korea University Business School
Expected graduation: February 2027

I study how firms organize research and development across geography and how this shapes innovation in emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence. My dissertation, advised by Professor Eonsoo Kim, uses the 2017 Transformer breakthrough to show when geographically dispersed R&D gives firms an advantage in AI innovation.

Before my doctoral studies I worked in strategy, pricing and marketing at Ericsson. I also lead government-funded policy research on regulatory innovation as Director at Kellis Inc.
